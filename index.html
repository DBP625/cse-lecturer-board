Salary reality — Lecturer grade

circulars print “attractive remuneration”; these are the numbers behind it
Top tier
৳ 48–75k /month entry

BRAC, NSU, IUB, EWU, UIU, AIUB. Glassdoor pegs a BRACU Lecturer at ~65k base (58–74k with extras, comp rated 4.3/5 from 34 reviews); a listings site cites IUB entry at 50–70k plus development funds, insurance, sabbaticals. NSU pays at the top of this band.

Established mid-tier
৳ 35–50k /month entry

AUST, UAP, Daffodil, Green, BUBT, Southeast, Stamford, Eastern, ULAB. Circulars say “negotiable”; Green’s spells out the pattern — medical, provident fund, two festival bonuses, Fri–Sat off, yearly review, and “initial higher salary for outstanding academic and research records”. That last clause is your CGPA + publication working for you.

Regional & newer
৳ 25–40k /month entry

Premier, Southern, Port City, CIU, USTC, BGC Trust, Metropolitan, Leading, NEUB, WUB — mostly “as per university pay scale”. Market-wide, salary surveys put the average BD lecturer near 38.5k with a spread of roughly 18–61k.

2 bonuses/yrTwo festival bonuses are standard everywhere; provident fund and gratuity usually unlock after confirmation (6–12 months probation).
+ side incomeEvening/weekend-program classes, exam-script remuneration, invigilation, and project supervision commonly add ৳5–20k a month on top of base at mid-tier universities.
Ladder payThe jumps are steep: BRACU’s Glassdoor gap is Lecturer ~65k → Senior Lecturer ~130k. Promotion, not negotiation, is where lecturer pay really moves.
Name a numberSince circulars hide pay, the viva board will ask your expectation. Anchor on your tier’s band — and quote its upper half if your CGPA and thesis publication back you up.

The bar, and your paperwork

what these circulars keep asking for
CGPA ≥ 3.50The standard floor at both B.Sc. and M.Sc. level (UIU, BUBT, SEU, Stamford, East Delta, Metropolitan). NEUB accepted 3.25. Top-tier boards say “excellent academic record” instead — they mean higher.
SSC/HSC ≥ 4.00Most circulars want GPA 4.00+ at both; BUBT asks 4.50. A few top boards quietly prefer straight 5.00s.
M.Sc. + papersA Master’s and at least one publication move you from “eligible” to “shortlisted” almost everywhere. Fresh B.Sc. graduates do get hired — on CGPA, thesis work, and referees.
0 yrs OKLecturer grade takes fresh graduates at most boards (UIU says it outright; Eastern “prefers” 2–3 yrs; experience is a tiebreaker, not a gate). The ladder after entry: Senior Lecturer ≈ 2 yrs + a journal paper (Stamford states it); Assistant Professor ≈ 3 yrs + ~3 recognized publications — 2 yrs with an M.Phil., 1 with a PhD, and some boards now demand Scopus-indexed. East Delta’s adjunct call is the fastest first classroom.
Age ≤ 35Appears at some boards (NEUB states it; others imply it for Lecturer grade). Not an issue now — just don’t sit on applications.
2 cycles/yrHiring clusters before semesters: roughly May–Aug for Fall intake, Oct–Jan for Spring. Missing a circular usually means a ~6-month wait, so the weekly check matters.
Sunday papersSeveral boards (Daffodil, Premier, NEUB) still print circulars in Prothom Alo / Ittefaq first. The feed trackers above catch most of these within a day or two.

Preparation — the four gates

how private-uni lecturer selection actually runs (from candidate interview reports: DIU, UIU, Green, BUBT, Eastern, Northern)
  1. ১

    Paper screen

    HR and the department head filter on the CGPA floors and file completeness before a human reads a word of your prose. Sloppy files die here, not at the viva.

    • Tailor the CV per university — name the exact courses you can teach from their catalogue (Programming, Discrete Math, DSA, DBMS, AI…).
    • Attach a publication list even if it holds one item; your thesis paper leads it.
    • Follow the circular’s format to the letter — bank draft, photo count, subject line. NEUB and Premier still want hard copies.
    • Referee #1 = your thesis supervisor, already warned they’ll be emailed.
  2. ২

    Written test

    Common at mid-tier boards; reported formats run ~40 marks subject + ~20 marks English. The subject part is 1st/2nd-year core — the courses they’re hiring you to teach.

    • Re-solve your own freshman/sophomore finals: C/OOP, data structures & algorithms, DBMS, operating systems, networking, discrete math, digital logic.
    • English section = grammar + short composition. Twenty easy marks if you don’t skip prep.
    • Top-tier boards often skip the written test and go straight to presentation + interview (UIU’s stated flow).
  3. ৩

    Demo class

    Five to twenty minutes teaching before the department — topic your choice or assigned from the freshman syllabus. This is the gate that actually decides most hires.

    • Structure that works: hook (why care) → definition → worked example on the board → recap + one question to the room.
    • Pick a topic you can teach with a marker, no slides needed — recursion, normalization, TCP handshake, binary search trees.
    • Deliver in English; most private classrooms run on it. Practice on juniors and time yourself — running over reads as unprepared.
    • Panels interrupt deliberately to test composure. Pause, answer, continue.
  4. ৪

    Viva board

    VC/Pro-VC, Dean, and Head across the table. Subject depth plus fit — and the salary question lands here.

    • Prepare crisp answers for: which courses can you take (say yes to labs), why teaching over industry, where’s your research going.
    • Your final-year research is the answer here — current, fundable, and demonstrably yours. Rehearse a crisp 2-minute version.
    • Carry the full hard-copy file: certificates SSC→B.Sc., NID, photos, publication reprints.
    • Salary expectation: name your tier’s band (see above), upper half if your record carries it.
MeanwhileBetween circulars: get the thesis paper submitted/published, set up Google Scholar + ORCID, keep LinkedIn current (universities do check), and plan the M.Sc. — the Assistant-Professor track needs it. Adjunct/TA posts (East Delta, open now) put “university teaching” on the CV within a semester.
